Place your sheet music PDF here as: sheet_music.pdf

The app will copy it to internal storage on first launch and render it page by page.

Gesture controls (GAME_ROTATION_VECTOR):
  - Quick head tilt RIGHT  →  next page
  - Quick head tilt LEFT   →  previous page

Tune the ANGULAR_VELOCITY_THRESHOLD constant in MainActivity.kt if the gesture
sensitivity needs adjustment (default: 1.8 rad/s).
